What a topic leaderboard is

Your competitors' videos, ranked by how far each one beat that creator's usual views. When a video does 10× the usual, the topic did the work — so you make your own take on it.

The score
Outlier scoreviews ÷ the creator's usual views (the median of their recent videos)
10×500K views on an account that usually gets 50K
Proventhe same topic spikes (3× or more) for 2+ creators
Maybe luckone creator, one spike — it could just be his audience
Copy the topic, not the video
Takethe topic, the hook pattern, the format
Leavetheir script, their footage, their face
Addyour example, your numbers, your city

Step 1 · both ways

Pick 10 creators

Your watchlist decides what the board can find. Keep it in your niche and your language.

Who to pick

  • 5 big names in your niche — they show which topics are hot.
  • 5 creators your size — a 10× hit there is a topic you can win, not just a famous face.
  • Same audience as yours: same niche, same language, same country if you can.
  • Mix platforms if you like — Reels, Shorts and TikTok all show views.
  • Swap out anyone off-topic every week or two.
Sandcastles says the same

"Don't overlook smaller creators: Smaller, niche creators often give more targeted and actionable ideas than large channels." — Sandcastles' watchlist guide. They suggest starting with 5–10 channels for a niche and growing to 30–40.

The $0 way

Screenshots + Claude

2

Screenshot each creator's video tab

Open their profile → Reels (or Shorts, or TikTok's videos). Every tile shows its views. Skip the pinned ones at the top — they're old hits. Take 2–3 screenshots each, about 30 videos.

3

Paste this into Claude with the screenshots

Copy this prompt — the $0 leaderboard
You are my content researcher. I'm attaching screenshots of the Reels / Shorts / TikTok tab of a few creators in my niche. Each tile shows a view count.

1. For each creator, read every tile's views and its cover text. Write "?" if a number is unreadable. Never guess.
2. Each creator's usual views = the median of their videos in my screenshots.
3. Outlier score = a video's views ÷ that creator's usual views.
4. Make a leaderboard of the top 10 by outlier score: rank, creator, what the video is about, views, their usual, outlier score (one decimal).
5. Group the top 10 into topics. A topic is PROVEN if it hits 3× or more for 2+ different creators, MAYBE LUCK if only one.
6. For each PROVEN topic: one line on why it worked (hook, format, timing), then 3 angles I could make for my audience: [who you make videos for]. My own take, never their script.

Show the leaderboard as a table, then the topics. Keep it short.

Before you trust the board, ask Claude to list the views it read, and fix any it misread. On my test it read 10 of 10 right.

When it goes wrong

Fixes for the usual problems

"I can't open Instagram"True — stock Claude can't. Use screenshots (the $0 way) or the Sandcastles connector.
Numbers look offAsk Claude to list every view it read. Fix the misreads, then rerun.
Old hits on topPinned posts. Skip them in your screenshots.
One creator wins everythingThat's one audience. Wait for a second creator before you film.
Sandcastles missing in ClaudeCheck you're on Pro or higher, then reconnect in Settings → Connectors (Claude Code: run /mcp).
Topics feel off-nicheYour watchlist is too wide. Swap in 5 creators your size.
