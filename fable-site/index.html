The exact prompt

Word for word what I sent. Claude Fable 5.2 in Claude Code 2.1.322, 10 Nov 2026.

Copy this prompt
Build a website for my design studio, Halfmoon Studio. We're a 3-person studio in Pune that designs brands and websites for small businesses.

One page with:
- a hero with our name and one line about what we do
- 6 selected projects in a grid (make the project images yourself with CSS or SVG, no stock photos)
- our services, with prices starting at ₹25,000
- a short about section with the team
- a contact form

Make it look like a real studio's website: a strong layout, smooth scroll animations, and real copy (no lorem ipsum).
It must work on a phone too.

Use plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in this folder. No build step and no libraries, except Google Fonts.
When it's done, open index.html in my browser.

The fill-in version

The same prompt with your details in the brackets. Delete the brackets when you fill them.

Fill in the brackets
Build a website for my [type of business], [business name]. We're [how many people] in [city] and we [what you do, and for whom].

One page with:
- a hero with our name and one line about what we do
- [number] [projects / products / dishes] in a grid (make the images yourself with CSS or SVG, no stock photos)
- our [services / menu], with prices starting at [your lowest price]
- a short about section with [the team / the owner]
- a contact form

Make it look like a real [type of business]'s website: a strong layout, smooth scroll animations, and real copy (no lorem ipsum).
It must work on a phone too.

Use plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in this folder. No build step and no libraries, except Google Fonts.
When it's done, open index.html in my browser.
  • Keep "real copy (no lorem ipsum)". It's why the site reads finished.
  • Give it real facts: your street, your prices, your hours. Whatever you leave out, it makes up.
  • No contact form? Ask for a WhatsApp button or a booking link instead.
Before it goes live

What to check before you ship

  • Scroll every section, slowly, and wait. Pictures that appear on scroll can stay empty. Opus's site from this same prompt looked done until I reached its six project pictures: they never showed up.
  • Open it on your phone. The prompt asks for it, so test it.
  • Read every word. It invents what you don't give it: Fable made up six client projects (Kaapi Katta, Ovi Dental Care…) and its own small print ("Prices are before GST"). Swap in your real work and terms.
  • Send yourself a test message. A plain HTML form needs somewhere to send to, like a form service or your email. If nothing arrives, ask Claude to connect it.
  • Click every link and menu item, then open it in a second browser.
