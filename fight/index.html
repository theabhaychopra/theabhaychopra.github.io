The fighters

Four agents in 10 weeks

Each one has its own computer in the cloud and can reach out to you without being asked. Here's who they are, in launch order.

Grok BotxAI (now SpaceXSI) · beta since 11 Aug 2026. A team of always-on Bots with their own cloud computer, in Grok's desktop and mobile apps, Slack, and @bot on X.
MuseMeta · 8 Sep 2026. A personal AI agent in the Muse app, on muse.ai and in WhatsApp, running on its own secure computer (Muse Spark model).
dotsOpenAI · 29 Sep 2026. "Your dot": an always-on agent inside ChatGPT (not a separate app), on GPT-6 Astra.
Claude DuetAnthropic · research preview since 21 Oct 2026. Its own app (plus claude.ai) that briefs you every morning, on Claude Opus 5.5.

Not to mix up: ChatGPT Pulse (retired June 2026), Grok Automations, the Meta AI app and Claude's scheduled tasks are different products.

Round 1

Who messages you first?

Claude DuetYes. A morning brief, then updates through the day. Checks your apps "every few hours"; no exact number is given.
MuseYes. "It can send you messages without you prompting it", on a schedule and when something new happens. You can dial it up or down.
dotsYes. Messages you "with progress, questions, or decisions that need you". It decides itself when to wake up and keep working.
Grok BotMostly when it needs you. Bots "only come back when something needs your approval"; since 2 Oct your main Bot can also offer to take work. It runs on schedules you set, at least 5 minutes apart.
Round 2

What it costs

US prices per month, from each maker's own pricing page. No official rupee prices are published; check yours at checkout.

MuseFree, with a usage limit. Power $20, Maximum $100.
Claude DuetIncluded with Claude Pro ($20, or $17 billed yearly) or Max, at no extra cost during the preview. Its check-ins use your normal Claude limits.
Grok BotFrom $30 (X Premium). Super $100, Ultra $200. Also included with paid Cursor and SuperGrok plans. X Plus doesn't include it.
dotsNeeds ChatGPT Pro: from $100 (Pro 100; also Pro 200 and Pro 500). Plus ($20) isn't eligible. Chats with your dot don't count toward your ChatGPT limits.
Round 3

Who can get it

Claude DuetUS only, by waitlist, Pro and Max. Team and Enterprise are "not included yet". No date for other countries.
MuseUS and Canada, 18 and over. "We don't have additional timing to share now."
dotsChatGPT Pro users 18+ in most countries: everywhere except the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, rolling out gradually.
Grok BotNo country limit listed for the app. Only tagging @bot on X is blocked in Australia, the UK, the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.
Round 4

The catch, and what to do about it

One honest catch each, from the maker's own help pages, plus the one thing I'd do.

MuseUses your chats to improve Meta's AI models; the setting "is on when you first use Muse". Do: Settings → Data controls → turn it off (it also covers past chats).
dots"You currently cannot view, delete, or directly modify individual dot memories." Do: keep sensitive projects out; deleting the dot is the only reset.
Grok Bot"All of your Bots use the same cloud computer", with shared files and logins. Approvals from unattended runs expire in ~10 minutes. Do: don't treat separate Bots as separate locked rooms.
Claude Duet"You can't watch Claude Duet work in its browser or take over the browser" (Muse and dots let you). Do: read its task list; it asks before it sends, pays, signs or deletes.
The verdict

No knockout

Each one wins a different job. Pick by what you need.

Free to startMuse: a free tier with a usage cap (US and Canada).
Spares your limitsdots: chatting with your dot doesn't count toward your ChatGPT limits (Pro).
Your own scheduleGrok Bot: you set every routine, down to 5-minute gaps.
Already on Claude ProClaude Duet: no extra cost during the preview (US waitlist).
