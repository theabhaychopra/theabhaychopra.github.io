Before you connect anything

Six questions, four agents

To message you first, an agent has to read your apps first. I asked the same six questions of each one, and answered them only from the maker's own help pages.

ReadsWhat can it open: your inbox, calendar, files, your own computer? What's on by default?
TrainsDo your chats and its work train the company's AI? Where is the switch?
RemembersWhat does it keep, can you see it, and what stays after you delete?
Asks firstWhich actions wait for your OK, and can you make it stricter?
Off switchHow do you disconnect an app, stop it, or wipe it?
Watch itCan you see what it's doing, or take over?

Menus move. Every path here was checked on 22 Oct 2026; if your screen differs, the official page linked at the end has the latest.

Muse · Meta

Muse trains on you by default

US and Canada only, 18+. Free with a usage limit.

ReadsFacebook, Instagram and Threads connect automatically if they share an Accounts Center with Muse. Gmail, calendar and other apps only if you add them. On a Mac you choose per app: Off, Read only or Read and interact.
TrainsOn from your first chat. Settings → Data controls → Help improve our AI models → off → Turn off. Meta says the change "also applies to previous interactions".
RemembersYour chats, files and a Memory.md you can read and edit: Assistant icon → Identity → Memory. Meta warns it "may still remember" what you delete.
Asks firstSettings → Permissions → Always ask (it asks before any action). Avoid "Always allow" on email.
Off switchSettings → Connectors to disconnect. Reset Muse (Settings → Data controls) deletes everything and can't be undone.
Watch itYes: Open browser, Take control, Stop the task, and an Activity log of what it did.
dots · OpenAI, inside ChatGPT

dots keeps its own notes

ChatGPT Pro (from $100 a month), most countries except the EEA, Switzerland and the UK.

ReadsThe apps you connect in ChatGPT, with the same permissions (you can allow read email without send). Access to your own computer starts turned off.
TrainsSettings → Data controls → Improve the model for everyone → off (on phone: profile icon → Settings → Data controls). Affects new chats. OpenAI doesn't train directly on its background research. Human review can still happen with this off.
RemembersIts own notes, plus your ChatGPT memory. Turning off ChatGPT memory doesn't wipe what it already has, and you can't view or edit its notes one by one.
Asks firstSettings → Personalization → Custom rules: take action, ask first, or hand off to you. Password changes and money transfers always come back to you.
Off switchIts profile → ••• → Pause. Its profile → ••• → Reset deletes its chats, memories and schedules. Disconnecting an app doesn't delete what it learned.
Watch itYes: open its computer, Take over, and Activity in the desktop app.
Grok Bot · xAI (now SpaceXSI)

All your Bots share one computer

From $30 a month, or included with paid Cursor plans. Its data settings live in your Cursor account.

ReadsEvery app you connect works for every Bot. All Bots share one cloud computer: files, browser sessions, logins. Your own computer: Settings → General → Bot → Execution on Local Computer, default Ask every time.
TrainsFollows your Cursor privacy settings (Cursor dashboard → Settings → Privacy). Grok Bot doesn't support Legacy Privacy Mode.
RemembersPreferences, facts and work summaries. No memory viewer is documented; correct it in chat.
Asks firstSettings → General → Auto-review → add Ask first rules (they win over Allow rules). Keep drafts on: "Disable drafts" lets a Bot send email and Slack directly. Approvals from unattended runs expire in about 10 minutes.
Off switchPer Bot: Notifications. Send "Stop now". Deleting a Bot can leave files and logins: sign out of sites, uninstall connectors, clear /workspace. A full wipe = delete your Cursor account (gone within 30 days).
Watch itYes: a live preview of the shared computer, Take control, and the chat shows every tool it used.
Claude Duet · Anthropic

You can't watch Duet work

A US-only research preview, by waitlist, on Claude Pro and Max.

ReadsThe connectors you've set up in Claude (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Slack and more) and your past Claude chats. Not the files on your computer.
TrainsNo Duet-only switch: it "uses your Claude account settings". Yours: Settings → Privacy → Help Improve our AI models. Claude doesn't train on raw connector content.
RemembersIt uses and adds to your Claude memory. Settings → Memory shows every memory: read, edit or delete each one. Deleting a chat doesn't delete memories made from it.
Asks firstIt shows you the exact action before it sends, pays, signs or deletes anything. Trading and moving money are off limits even if you approve.
Off switchSettings → Memory → Search and reference chats off stops it reading old chats. Customize → Connectors to disconnect (what it already learned stays in memory).
Watch itNo live view or take-over of its browser. The Tasks tab shows what it's working on and what needs you.
All four

Delete doesn't mean forgotten

Disconnecting an app stops new reading. What the agent already learned usually stays. Here's what each wipe really removes.

MuseDeleting a message or file: it "may still remember" what it learned. Reset Muse deletes everything. Turning training off also covers past chats.
dotsDisconnecting an app keeps what it learned. Reset deletes its chats, memories and schedules, but not the files, Codex threads or ChatGPT chats it created.
Grok BotDeleting a Bot removes its chat and routines; files and logins on the shared computer can stay. Clean them up, or delete the Cursor account.
Claude DuetDeleting a chat removes it from search, not the memories made from it. Delete those one by one in Settings → Memory, or Reset memory.

Switches change what's used from now on. Anthropic says data already used in training stays in those models; Muse is the one whose switch also covers your past chats.

Freelancers

Your clients are in there too

Connect an inbox and every person in it comes along. Meta's own words: "information about you may be provided to Muse, even if you don't use Muse directly."

My ruleAsk a client before you connect an inbox or drive that holds their work.
Read-onlyWhere it's offered: Muse connectors can be read-only; ChatGPT app permissions can allow reading email without sending.
BystandersMeta has a page for people who don't use Muse. OpenAI takes privacy requests at its Privacy Portal or dsar@openai.com, even without an account.
Never pastePasswords or ID numbers into a chat. Each agent has a secure sign-in that keeps your password away from the AI.
Bonus

Ask it what it remembers

Paste this into any assistant with memory. Answers differ, and a dot can't show you its own notes, so treat the reply as a starting point.

Copy this prompt
List everything you remember about me: facts, preferences, people I've mentioned, and anything you learned from my connected apps. Group it by topic and say where each item came from (a chat, an app, or a file). Don't add guesses. Then tell me exactly where in your settings I can view, edit or delete each kind of memory.
Then

Delete what you don't want it to keep, using the paths in this guide. For Claude, Settings → Memory lists every memory directly.
