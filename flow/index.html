Before you start

What you need

  • A Google account — Flow is for ages 18+ and works in India.
  • Google Flow — a website, nothing to install: labs.google/flow. Phone browser or laptop.
  • One clear photo of you — the next section says which kind.
  • Free credits — Flow gives everyone 50 credits a day. One 10-second video is 15.
How it works

Flow doesn't have a "10 poses" button. You do it in two moves: first Flow makes one image of you in 10 poses, then it animates that image so every pose moves on its own.

Step 0

Pick the right photo

The #1 complaint on this trend is "my face changed". Most of the time, the photo is why.

Use a photo that is
Front-facingboth eyes visible, looking at the camera
Sharp, well litdaylight from a window is perfect
Unfilteredno beauty mode, no smoothing, no stickers
Just younever other people's photos, and no children
Waist-upor full body, on a plain wall — the poses need your body too
Face clearno sunglasses, no cap covering your face
Why faces drift

A filtered selfie gives Flow a face that isn't quite yours, a side angle hides half of it, and a tiny low-res photo leaves it guessing. Start sharp and straight-on, and the face-lock lines do the rest.

1

Upload your photo

Open labs.google/flow → New project → upload your photo into it.

2

Make one image of you in 10 poses

Prompt 1 — image (Nano Banana, 9:16), your photo attached
Use my uploaded photo as the only reference for this person.
Make ONE vertical 9:16 image: a clean grid of 2 columns × 5 rows — 10 panels of the SAME man, full body, in 10 different poses (the list is below).
Keep his exact face, hairstyle, beard, skin tone and body shape from my photo in every panel. Do not beautify, slim or change him.
Same outfit in all 10 panels: black t-shirt, dark jeans, white sneakers.
Plain white studio background, soft even light, thin white gaps between panels. No text, no numbers, no borders.

Make 2 and keep the one where every face looks like you. If one panel looks like someone else, generate again — don't animate it.

Add to prompt 1
Poses, left to right, top to bottom:
1. standing straight, hands in pockets
2. arms crossed, confident smile
3. walking towards the camera
4. looking back over his shoulder
5. sitting on a stool, leaning forward
6. adjusting his watch
7. hand on his chin, thinking
8. laughing, head tilted back
9. leaning on a wall, one foot up
10. side profile, looking into the distance
Prompt 1, your way

Make it yours

Change the words, keep the face-lock lines.

Swap in your version
Women's versionwrite woman instead of man, and her instead of his
Traditional outfitdescribe it in the outfit line — a dark green silk saree with gold border and jhumkas — and add keep the saree and jewellery identical
Fewer posesa 2 × 3 grid of 6 poses gives each face more pixels — easier to keep it yours
Your businessa product in your hand, your shop as the background, a uniform as the outfit
Make 2, keep the best

Generate the image twice and keep the one where every face looks like you. A wrong face in the image stays wrong in the video.

3

Animate it

Pick Frames to Video and set your 10-pose image as the first frame.

Prompt 2 — video
Animate this image. It is a grid of 10 panels of the same man. Animate every panel on its own, with small natural movements that fit its pose — a step, a turn of the head, a smile, a breath, a hand moving.
Keep his exact face and outfit from the image in every frame. No face changes, no morphing, no new people.
The grid, the white gaps and the background stay perfectly still: static camera, no zoom, no pan, no transitions, no text.
Realistic, smooth motion for the full 10 seconds.

The fix nobody shows

The face-lock lines

These lines are already in both prompts. If your face still drifts, check they're there, word for word.

Keep these in every prompt
Keep his exact face, hairstyle, beard, skin tone and body shape from my photo.
Do not beautify, slim or change him.
No face changes, no morphing, no new people.
Static camera, no zoom.
Still changing? Try, in this order
1A sharper, front-facing photo with no filter
2Fewer poses — 6 instead of 10, so each face is bigger
3Smaller movements — "a breath, a small smile", not "dancing"
4360p first to test cheaply, then 720p once the faces hold
5Regenerate the image before you animate it
Bonus

One pose, full screen

The grid is the hook. One pose on its own, full screen, is the clip you'll actually post.

Prompt 3a — image, your 10-pose image attached
From this grid, take pose 3 and make it a single full-frame vertical 9:16 photo of him in that pose. Same face, same outfit, same white studio, full body, sharp. Keep his exact face from my photo.
Prompt 3b — Frames to Video, 9:16, 8 s
Animate this photo: he walks two steps towards the camera and smiles. Keep his exact face and outfit. Static camera, no zoom. Realistic, smooth motion.

Good to know

Before you post

Evening rushwithout a subscription you may not be able to make videos from about 7:30 to 10:30 PM IST (2–5 PM UTC). Make them in the morning.
Watermarkvideos made in India get a small visible Google watermark, plus an invisible SynthID one. Only the top Ultra plan removes the visible one.
Couldn't generateFlow filters uploaded photos of people. Try a clearer photo of yourself — never someone else's, never a child's.
Phone troubleuse Chrome, or a laptop. The Flow Agent (asking for variations in chat) is web-only.
Client workGoogle says it won't claim ownership of what you make — read Flow's terms first.
AI labelthese are AI videos made from your photo — turn on Instagram's AI label when you post.
