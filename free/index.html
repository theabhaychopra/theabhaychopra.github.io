Voice cloning

Chatterbox

instead of ElevenLabs $6/mo → $0

Clones your own voice from a short clip and reads any script in it — with a dedicated Hindi model.

Free limitUnlimited on your laptop (every file carries an inaudible watermark). In the browser: about 2 GPU-minutes a day logged out, 5 with a free Hugging Face account.
Runs onAny laptop — the small Nano model runs on a normal CPU.
Paid still winsA ready-made voice library and an API.
Client workMIT — fine for client work. Clone only your own voice.
Start in 3 steps
  1. Open the Hindi demo in your browser and upload 10–20 seconds of your voice.
  2. Type your script (Hindi or English) and generate.
  3. Like it? Install it from GitHub to run it free and unlimited.
Try in browser ↗huggingface.coGitHub ↗github.com
Photo upscaling

Upscayl

instead of Topaz Gigapixel $149/yr → $0

Makes blurry or low-res photos about 4× sharper, on your own computer.

Free limitUnlimited, offline, no watermark.
Runs onA computer with a Vulkan graphics card — most built-in laptop GPUs can't run it.
Paid still winsVideo. Upscayl does photos only.
Client workOpen source (AGPL) — your images are yours.
Start in 3 steps
  1. Download it from upscayl.org or GitHub — the Mac App Store copy costs money, this one doesn't.
  2. Drop in a photo and pick a model (the default for photos, Digital Art for graphics).
  3. Hit Upscayl and save.

Background removal

Remove Background

instead of remove.bg $9/mo → $0

Cuts the subject out of a photo in one click and saves a transparent PNG — no upload, no account. Already built into your laptop.

Free limitUnlimited, full resolution, no watermark.
Runs onmacOS (Preview or Finder) and Windows 11 (Paint).
Paid still winsHundreds of photos at once, through its API.
Client workBuilt into your system — use it for anything.
Start in 3 steps
  1. Mac: open the photo in Preview → Tools → Remove Background — or right-click it in Finder → Quick Actions → Remove Background.
  2. Windows 11: open it in Paint → Remove background.
  3. Save as PNG to keep the transparent background.
Apple's guide ↗support.apple.comrembg (for bulk) ↗github.com
AI music

ACE-Step 1.5

instead of Suno Pro ~$10/mo → $0

Full songs with vocals and lyrics from one prompt — 10 seconds to 10 minutes, in 50+ languages.

Free limitUnlimited on your machine. In the browser: a daily GPU quota.
Runs onA graphics card with 4 GB of memory, or an Apple Silicon Mac.
Paid still winsOne click in the browser, nothing to install.
Client workMIT — fine for client work.
Start in 3 steps

Good to know: Only download it from that GitHub page — fake "ACE-Step" sites exist.

Captions

Buzz

instead of Descript $24/mo → $0

Offline Whisper transcription → caption files (SRT, VTT, TXT). Hindi included.

Free limitUnlimited, offline, no account.
Runs onAny Mac, Windows or Linux laptop.
