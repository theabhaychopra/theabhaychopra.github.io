Why this is happening

Your reviews live on one site

Almost all of your reviews are on Google. ChatGPT mostly looks elsewhere, so it can't see how good you are.

Your details don't match

Phone numbers and addresses differ from site to site, and two stores are missing from your own website. That confuses AI.

Your website says very little

There's no page for each store and no answers to common questions. AI quotes websites that explain things clearly.

Nobody is writing about you

Local "best of" lists and forums mention your competitors. AI repeats what other people say about a store.
